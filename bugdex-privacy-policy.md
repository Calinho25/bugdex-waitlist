# Bugdex Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 23, 2026

Bugdex is a mobile app for identifying insects and arthropods, recording sightings, building a personal collection, and taking part in optional discovery features.

This policy explains what information Bugdex collects, how it is used, and the choices available to you.

## Contact

If you have questions about this policy or want to request account or data deletion, contact:

caluweezi@gmail.com

## Information We Collect

### Account Information

If you create an account, Bugdex may collect your email address, user ID, display name, profile details, profile photo, subscription status, achievements, quests, group memberships, leaderboard progress, and app settings.

### Photos And Capture Information

Bugdex lets you take or choose insect photos for identification. These photos may be stored privately with your account so your capture history, collection, and identification results can be shown in the app.

Capture records may include species candidates, confidence scores, capture time, rarity, notes, favourites, moderation status, and related app progress.

### Location Information

Bugdex may ask for foreground location permission when you choose to enable location-based features. Location is optional. If enabled, location may be used to improve identification context, show nearby sightings, personalize local discovery guidance, and support location-based achievements.

Bugdex does not request background location access and does not continuously track your location.

Exact capture coordinates are treated as private account data. If you choose to share a sighting publicly, Bugdex uses a privacy-protected approximate location instead of publishing exact coordinates.

### Purchases And Subscription Status

Bugdex uses Google Play billing and RevenueCat to manage premium access. Bugdex may receive and store subscription status, entitlement state, transaction-related identifiers, renewal state, expiry state, and related purchase metadata needed to keep premium access working.

### Device, Diagnostics, And App Activity

Bugdex may collect app activity and technical data needed to run the service, troubleshoot problems, prevent abuse, maintain security, and improve reliability. This can include device or app diagnostics, crash information, feature usage, upload status, retry state, and authentication/session metadata.

## How We Use Information

Bugdex uses information to:

- identify insects and return species results;
- save your private capture history and collection;
- provide achievements, quests, rarity reveals, leaderboards, groups, and other app progress;
- manage premium access and subscription status;
- personalize local discovery guidance when location is enabled;
- protect exact location and private photos;
- prevent duplicate uploads, abuse, and unauthorized access;
- troubleshoot errors and improve app reliability;
- respond to support, privacy, and deletion requests.

## Service Providers

Bugdex uses trusted service providers to operate the app:

- Supabase for authentication, database, private storage, server functions, and security controls;
- Kindwise insect.id for insect identification from submitted capture photos and related context;
- RevenueCat for subscription entitlement management;
- Google Play for Android app distribution, purchases, and billing.

These providers process information only as needed to provide their services to Bugdex.

## Sharing

Bugdex does not sell personal information.

Bugdex does not use third-party advertising SDKs and does not use the Android Advertising ID.

Bugdex may share information when needed to operate the app, comply with law, prevent abuse, protect users, or process information through the service providers listed above.

Public-facing features are designed to avoid exposing exact coordinates, private storage paths, email addresses, private photos, or account identifiers. Some profile, collection, group, leaderboard, or public sighting information may be visible to other users if you choose to use those features.

## Data Security

Bugdex uses authentication, row-level access controls, private storage, server-owned mutations, and encrypted network connections to help protect user data. No system can be guaranteed completely secure, but Bugdex is designed to keep private captures and exact locations restricted to the account owner and trusted server processes.

## Data Retention And Deletion

Bugdex keeps account, capture, collection, subscription, and progress data while your account is active or as needed to provide the service, meet legal requirements, resolve disputes, prevent abuse, and maintain security.

You can request account or data deletion by contacting caluweezi@gmail.com. Some records may be retained where required for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, billing records, or backup retention.

## Children

Bugdex is intended for users aged 13 and older. Bugdex is not directed to children under 13.

## International Use

Bugdex may process information in countries where Bugdex and its service providers operate. By using Bugdex, you understand that your information may be processed outside your country of residence.
